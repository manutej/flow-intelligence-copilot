What FlowIQ can answer

This is a demo portfolio, not your data. Type a question in the command bar and FlowIQ answers it from the exports below, citing the rows behind every figure. If the data can't answer a question, it says so.

The data

8 operating buildings, 1,100 units, data as of 2026-10-01.

  • Halsted & Lake P01
  • Marlowe Residences P02
  • Fulton Yards P03
  • The Clarendon P04
  • Armitage Commons P05
  • Kedzie Works P06
  • Printers Row Tower P07
  • Wabash Point P08

Data domains

  • properties and units (PMS: data/pms/properties.csv, data/pms/units.csv; 1,100 unit rows)
  • leases and renewals (PMS: data/pms/leases.csv; 1,033 leases)
  • leads, tours, applications and signed leases by source (CRM: data/crm/leads.csv; 1,498 leads)
  • marketing spend by source (ILS ad billing: data/ils/spend.csv; 208 rows)
  • past budget decisions (data/ops/decisions.csv; 10 rows)

Example questions

  • How many vacant units are at Kedzie Works?
  • Which source has the highest cost per lead in Q3 2026?
  • What was total marketing spend in Q2 2026?
  • How many leases expire at Kedzie Works in 9/2026?

What it can't do

You can't upload a rent roll, connect a property management system, or add your own buildings on this site.
