The Field Notebook · Vol. I I
We built FlowIQ as if it were Week 1 on the job with real capital on the line: never ask a language model to do floating-point joins in token space or read unreviewed CRM notes beside system instructions.

Multifamily portfolio intelligence fails when an AI copilot eyeballs raw CSV dumps or accumulates brittle per-question regex patches. Our architecture enforces a strict unidirectional dependency graph (config -> data -> domain -> agent -> mcp/cli/web/temporal) that separates a pure deterministic domain engine from compositional query planning and multi-provider delivery shells.

Every domain rule—from the 1 CAD = 0.73 USD exchange rate and status = void invoice gauge to the occupied + notice denominator and Empirical-Bayes prior beta0 = $3,250—lives in a single policy contract (src/config/rules.ts). Changing one policy knob recomputes all 3,858 rows, JSON results, and prose explanations in lockstep in under 5 milliseconds.

/ Plate V · FLOW-105 Closed-Loop Empirical-Bayes Learner ◆ law · Conjugate Gamma-Poisson Shrinkage ✓ accept · Window Invariance Verified

Guarding against small-sample Poisson noise with conjugate shrinkage.

The Field Notebook · Vol. I V
In apartment leasing, a single ad channel at one building signs 0 to 2 leases per month—so a short two-month window with zero leases is often Poisson luck, not proof that the channel broke.

Look at decisions:X009 (2026-08, P01 Halsted & Lake, Facebook Ads, decrease -$200/mo, accepted = yes): after the August cut, P01 Facebook Ads spent only $950 across 2 months (Aug–Sep) and signed 0 leases (having signed 1 lease on $2,390 in Apr–Jul). Under the portfolio prior (beta0 = $3,250/lease), expected leases in that $950 window is lambda = 950 / 3250 = 0.292, so the Poisson probability of seeing zero leases purely by chance is Pr(K=0) = exp(-0.292) = 74.7%!

By contrast, decisions:X001 (2026-05, P01 Zillow, increase +$300/mo, accepted = yes) burned $8,170 over 5 post-decision months ($9,690 total) with 0 leases (Pr(K=0) = exp(-8170/3250) = 8.1%). Conjugate Gamma-Poisson updating (Posterior CPSL = (S_pre + S_post + beta0) / (K_pre + K_post + alpha0)) protects X009 at $3,295.00 (rank #2 at P01) while penalizing X001 to $12,940.00 (rank #4, full cut) and promoting X007 (P06 Zillow) from #4 ($6,200.00) -> #2 ($3,233.33).
